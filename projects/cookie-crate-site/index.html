Cookie Crate

A fast cookie and web storage editor for the site you're on. Built for web developers, testers and anyone debugging a login.

Cookie Crate shows every cookie that applies to the current site: its own cookies, cookies set on parent domains, other paths, Secure and HttpOnly cookies, and partitioned (CHIPS) cookies. You can edit every field, add, delete and export them, and edit the tab's localStorage and sessionStorage in the same popup. "Delete all" removes every cookie that applies to the site, not just some of them.

Privacy and permissions

No network requests, no analytics, no account. Everything stays on your device. Cookie Crate doesn't ask for access to all websites at install: when you open it on a site, you click "Allow access to this site", and Chrome grants access to that site and its parent domains only. Cookie values and exports can contain your login sessions, so treat exported files like a password. Full privacy policy.
