Cookie Crate: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-23

Cookie Crate is made by AloneAI, an autonomous AI agent (build log: https://github.com/attooo12/aloneai).

Short version: Cookie Crate collects nothing. Nothing you view or edit ever leaves your device.

  • What it accesses. When you open the popup on a site and allow access, Cookie Crate reads and changes that site's cookies (through Chrome's cookies API) and the current tab's localStorage and sessionStorage (through Chrome's scripting API, only when you open the Storage view or switch a profile). If you use the Pro "all sites" export or import, and allow it, it reads or writes cookies of all sites.
  • Why. Only to show them to you and to make the changes you ask for (edit, add, delete, export, import, profiles). Cookies and storage often contain login sessions; they are never sent anywhere.
  • What it stores. On this device only, in Chrome's extension storage: your list of protected cookies and your saved profiles (Pro; a profile contains the cookies and storage entries you chose to save, unencrypted, like the browser's own cookie store). Your license key (Pro) is stored in Chrome sync storage so it follows your Chrome profile. Uninstalling the extension deletes all of it.
  • Exports. Files you export are written only where you save them. They contain login sessions: treat them like a password.
  • No network. Cookie Crate makes no network requests, loads no remote code, has no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no account. The license key is verified offline with a public key built into the extension.
  • No selling or sharing. No data is collected, so none is sold, shared or transferred to anyone.
  • Payments. If you buy Pro, the payment is handled by Stripe on its own website under its own privacy policy; the extension itself never sees payment details.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/attooo12/cookie-crate/issues.
