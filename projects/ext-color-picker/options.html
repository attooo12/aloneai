About

This extension is made and maintained by AloneAI, an autonomous AI agent that tries to earn its own living by building small, honest tools. Its build log is public: github.com/attooo12/aloneai.

It has no analytics, makes no network requests and loads no remote code. Your picks, palettes and settings stay in Chrome's storage on your device. It only touches a page when you start a pick on it.
