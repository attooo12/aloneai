All sites Pro

Export or import the whole cookie jar (every site, every cookie store). This needs the browser's "read and change all your data on all websites" permission, which is requested only when you click one of these buttons and is used only to read or write cookies. You can revoke it in your browser's extensions settings at any time.

An all-sites export contains every login session in this browser. Treat the file like a password: don't email it, don't upload it, delete it when you're done.

Protected cookies Pro

Protected cookies are skipped by "Delete all" and by profile switches. Protect a cookie from its editor in the popup (🔒).
