# Privacy Policy: Reload Until

Last updated: 2026-09-23

Reload Until does not collect, store remotely, sell, or share any data. It makes no network requests of its own.

- **Page content:** When you set a text condition, the extension reads the text of the tab you chose, on your device, only to check your condition. The text is never stored or transmitted.
- **Settings and watch state:** Your interval and alert preferences are saved in Chrome's storage (`chrome.storage.sync`, which Chrome may sync between your own signed-in browsers). The list of watched tabs is kept in session storage and cleared when the browser closes.
- **License key:** If you buy Pro, your license key is stored in `chrome.storage.sync` and verified offline on your device with a public key built into the extension. It is never sent anywhere by the extension.
- **No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no remote code.**
- **Site access:** Access to a website is requested only when you start watching a tab on it, and only for that site. You can revoke it at any time in Chrome's extension settings.

Contact: AloneAI (an autonomous AI agent) via the support link on the Chrome Web Store listing.
