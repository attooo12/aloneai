1. What Easy Auto Refresh is, as of September 2026

Easy Auto Refresh (by KSoft) is a Chrome extension that reloads a tab on a timer. Checked directly against its Chrome Web Store listing on September 24, 2026: version 6.5, built on Manifest V3, rated 3.9 out of 5 from roughly 2,000 ratings, with over 1,000,000 users installed.

Its free tier refreshes a page on a timer. According to the developer's own site, dummysoftware.com, unlocking the advanced options (per-URL/per-domain settings, random intervals, scheduled times, reloading every tab in a window, auto-clicking an element, and more) requires a paid license, and that license is a yearly subscription, not a one-time purchase: the page lists a Personal License at $29.95/year (or $3.95/month), an Educational License at $19.95/year, and a Business License at $54.95/year, explicitly described as "a subscription for 1 year per-user."

2. What users say about the pricing change

  • "Outrageous license costs for a tiny app." — David Lewis, 2026-01-26

Not every review is negative — the same page also has reviews praising the free tier's basic reload-on-a-timer behaviour, and it remains a widely used, 1,000,000+ user extension. The pattern in recent reviews is specifically about the subscription requirement for anything beyond the basic timer, and about cancellation being hard to find, not about the core free feature failing to work.

4. Where Easy Auto Refresh is better

It has a nine-year-plus track record and a much larger install base (1M+) than a brand-new extension like Reload Until. If all you want is dead-simple timer refresh on a single page and you don't mind the free-tier limits, it does that job, and its free tier costs nothing. Its per-site settings memory and scheduled-time options (once unlocked) go beyond what Reload Until offers, since Reload Until is built around "reload until a condition is true" rather than a general reload scheduler.

FAQ

Is Easy Auto Refresh free?

The basic timer-reload feature is free. Advanced options (multiple tabs at once, per-site settings, scheduled times, auto-click) require a paid license that, per the developer's own site, is billed yearly rather than as a one-time purchase.

How much does Easy Auto Refresh's paid tier cost?

As listed on dummysoftware.com on September 24, 2026: $19.95/year (educational), $29.95/year or $3.95/month (personal), $54.95/year (business), described as a per-user annual subscription.

Are there free alternatives with no subscription?

Yes: a manual refresh, a small userscript (see our walkthrough), or Reload Until's free tier (one tab, no account, no subscription). Reload Until's Pro tier is a single €9 payment rather than a recurring fee.

Does Reload Until do everything Easy Auto Refresh does?

No. Reload Until is built around watching for a text change and alerting you, not general per-site scheduling or auto-clicking page elements. If you need those specific features, Easy Auto Refresh or Auto Refresh Plus may fit better; check their current pricing before subscribing.
