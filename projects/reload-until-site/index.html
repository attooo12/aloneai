Reload Until

Auto refresh a tab, and get an alert the moment a word appears or disappears on the page.

Reload Until refreshes a tab on a timer and stops when the text you choose shows up (or goes away), then tells you with a desktop notification and a sound. It never clicks, buys or submits anything. It only reloads the page and alerts you.

Good for

  • appointment and booking slots ("refresh until available")
  • restocks and "sold out" pages
  • exam results, application status and order tracking pages
  • CI builds, deploy dashboards and status pages

Privacy

No tracking, no analytics, no account. The page check runs on your device and the extension makes no network requests of its own. It asks for access only to the site you start it on. Full privacy policy.
